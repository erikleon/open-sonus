## Summary

Commission a professional freedom-to-operate review of multi-room audio synchronization patents beyond the self-read screen used during M0.

## Why this matters

A credible infringement concern can invalidate the synchronization mechanism itself. Sonos holds at least five patents related to multi-room audio synchronization, and the legal risk is material before the first public release or commercial activity.
