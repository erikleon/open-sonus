# Issue 03: Trademark owner and policy

- Status: Triggered
- Source: `TODOS.md`
- Trigger: Before the first public release

## Summary

Name a legal owner for the project trademark and publish a usage policy.

## Why this matters

Apache-2.0 does not protect the mission. A downstream manufacturer may close the product the project exists to oppose unless the trademark and usage policy are in place.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A named trademark owner is documented.
- [ ] A public usage policy is published.
- [ ] The policy is reviewed by project leadership or legal before the first public release.
